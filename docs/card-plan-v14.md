# Card plan v14: rewards-track cards (draft 2)

Status: design draft, not implemented or simulated. Draft 3 includes B's feedback from 10 Nov (two rounds). Stats are deliberately uneven: each card's shape (glass cannon, wall, small engine) follows its role, not a tier average.

Wording: **Promote** = going up a tier in the shop. **Level up** = the rewards track only.

## Track structure (45 levels)

- **Levels 1–7: cards for the existing styles** (each tribe's tier 2 and 3 card). They plug straight into styles new players are still learning, so they are useful from the first run.
- **Level 10: the first set of legendaries**, one for every tribe (the 7 single-tribe legendaries). This replaces the 1,000-rating unlock.
- **New styles arrive as "spotlights":** three levels close together unlock all six cards of one new style (tiers 1+2, 3+4, 5+6). A style is playable as soon as its spotlight ends, instead of being spread over 30 levels.
- **Spotlight order goes from simple to complex,** and anything that builds on another mechanic comes after it: Gig Economy (teaches Side Gig) → Livestreamers → Hibernators → Microtransactions (uses Side Gig) → Moshers → Minimalists → Remix (copies other cards, so it's most fun once the pool is big).
- **Dual-tribe legendaries every 4 levels from 17 to 45.** Each one comes after the styles it bridges have unlocked.
- **The strongest build-arounds come late:** Crossover Hit, Mashup Artist and Cover Band are the finale.

## Punks: **Moshers** (new direction, draft 3)

*Friendly fire. In the pit your own minions bruise each other, and surviving a bruise makes them stronger. Cleave stays rare (only Mosh Pit and the Cover Band legendary).*

**Problem in draft 2:** Crust Punk (+2 to Punks' start-of-combat damage) and Mohawk Legend (Punks' start-of-combat effects trigger twice) also boosted the Moshers' hits on their own board, so Crowd Surfer or Festival Pit could wipe it.

**Fix: one new keyword, Bruise.** *Bruise (N): deal N damage to a friendly minion. It can't drop below 1 Health.*
- A bruise never kills, so damage boosts can't wreck your board. Crust Punk only adds to damage dealt to enemies (its text becomes "Your Punks' start-of-combat damage to enemy minions is increased by 2").
- Mohawk Legend stays as it is: double start-of-combat effects means double bruises and double payoffs. That is a deliberate link between Sabotage and Moshers, and a combo to measure.
- The keyword is universal, so other tribes can use it later (a Stoic that bruises itself, for example). Stoics' "after this survives damage" triggers on a bruise.

## Gold-loop check (card text)

I went through every card that makes gold, refunds gold, lowers a cost or makes copies, old and new, and looked for combinations that repeat without a limit.

### Endless loops found (all fixed above)

| Combo | How it loops | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Art Generator + AI Art Generator (draft 2 text) | Play the copy from your hand, its First Impression copies the AI Art Generator on your board, sell the one you played for 1 gold, play the new copy, repeat. Endless gold and endless "after you play" triggers. | AI Art Generator now makes one copy at **end of turn** (not a First Impression), and copies sell for 0 gold. |
| AI Art Generator + Wedding Band | Wedding Band re-triggers AI Art Generator's First Impression, which copies Wedding Band, and so on. | Fixed by the same change: there's no First Impression left to re-trigger. |
| AI Art Generator + Encore | Sell Encore: copy of AI Art Generator; play it: copy Encore; sell Encore… | Fixed by the same change. Encore also can't copy Encore. |
| Two Cover Bands next to each other (not gold, but endless) | Each one triggers the other's start-of-combat effect forever. | **Engine rule: a re-triggered effect can't re-trigger anything.** This also covers chains of Tribute Acts and Wedding Bands. |
| Two Mashup Artists next to each other | Each has the other's effects, which include the first one's: endless recursion. | Mashup Artist ignores other Mashup Artists. |

### Gold engines with a limit (fine, but strong; balance-test them)

| Engine | Most it makes |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment Agency + Unpaid Intern | 2 Interns a turn, each sells for 2: +4 gold a turn |
| Data Broker + any cost-1 Side Gig | Gigs refund themselves, so they're effectively free (twice per turn) |
| Ghost Kitchen + App Store + Data Broker + Double Shift | Discover for 1 gold, refunded next turn: about +2 to +4 gold a turn |
| Cashback Card (+ Consultant) | +2 gold a turn |
| Bootleg Merch + Greatest Hits + Gig Poster (+ Reunion Tour) | Sold Punks come back to the Feed, but rebuying them costs more than selling earns: about +2 gold a turn, at most |
| Magpie | One free Scroll a turn; several Magpies don't stack |
| Pop-Up Ad, Donation Goal, Sponsored Streamer | Fixed amounts per turn or per combat |

### Rules that keep it that way

- **Golden copies double the numbers, never the limits.** "Twice per turn" stays twice per turn on a Golden card.
- **Costs never go below 1 gold** (hiring, Scrolls, Side Gigs; Magpie's free first Scroll is the one exception). No 0-cost cards or abilities.
- **Copies made by card effects sell for 0 gold** (AI Art Generator, and any later card that copies).

## Balance watch list

- **Hibernating Bear:** at most 3 doublings (×8) per game. Check the ceiling when it has been buffed heavily before doubling.
- **Platform Monopoly** (whole board, permanent) with Double Shift and App Store: three Side Gigs a turn means +6/+6 to the whole board each turn.
- **Crossover Hit:** every Creative effect suddenly hits every minion. Likely the strongest card in the set.
- **Plagiarist** (permanent): snowballs off big enemy minions. The fallback is "for this combat".
- **Sound System, Capsule Wardrobe, Breathwork Coach, Ghostwriter:** caps removed; measure their late-game ceilings.
- **The Void:** +4/+4 per empty space on 3 minions is +16/+16 each. Strong by design; check the 1st-place rate.
- **Bot Farm** (2 Followers, 3 times per game) together with Giveaway Host and Engagement Farmer.
- **Data Centre** (now uncapped) and the gold engines (Pop-Up Ad, Data Broker, Cashback Card, Bootleg Merch): gold loops are hard for bots to value. Test by hand.
- **Mashup Artist:** still the highest build-around ceiling.
